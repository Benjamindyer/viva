Emergency Pet Transport

When plans change and timing is critical, Jon can arrange short-notice dog transport between Spain and the UK — at a custom price tailored to your situation.

Our regular service runs on scheduled routes at a fixed price — but life doesn't always work to a timetable. If you need to move your dog urgently, whether that's a sudden relocation, a family emergency, or paperwork that landed sooner than expected, Jon may be able to help with an emergency trip. Pricing depends on timing, route, and what's needed to make it happen safely.

Case study

Jenny & Rox — a short-notice move to Spain

Jenny had been planning a move to Spain with her dog Rox, but the timeline shifted suddenly when her rental in the UK came to an end earlier than expected. With just days to get Rox across before she flew out herself, the usual booking window wasn't an option.

She got in touch with Jon to explain the situation. He talked her through what was still needed on the paperwork side, checked whether Rox's travel documents were in order, and worked out whether a slot could be found on an upcoming crossing. Because the timing was tight, the trip was priced individually rather than at the standard fixed rate.

Rox travelled safely to Spain on schedule, and Jenny was able to focus on the rest of the move knowing her dog was in good hands. If you're facing a similar situation, get in touch — Jon will be honest about what's possible and what it will cost.
